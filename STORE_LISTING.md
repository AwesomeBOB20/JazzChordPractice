## Full description (max 4000 characters)
```
Kordal makes chords, scales, and music theory make sense — for guitar AND piano. See exactly where to put your fingers, hear how everything sounds, and build real musical skills. No music background needed.

Whether you're a beginner learning your first chords or a player digging into jazz voicings, Kordal shows you the fretboard and the keys side by side, with color-coded notes so every chord finally clicks.

EXPLORE ANY CHORD
• See any chord on guitar and piano with clear, color-coded diagrams
• Hear how each chord — and each individual note — actually sounds
• Step through positions and voicings up and down the neck
• Switch between guitar and piano instantly

VOICINGS & THE DICTIONARY
• Drop 2, drop 3, shell, and triad voicings across every string set
• Browse a full dictionary of chords, scales, arpeggios, and intervals
• Compare shapes side by side to really understand how they connect

BUILD SONGS
• Lay out chord progressions one measure at a time
• Play them back with real feel — straight, swing, bossa nova, reggae and more
• Add bass and a metronome, loop sections, and practice along at any tempo

TEST YOURSELF
• Quiz mode: see a chord shape and name it, or listen and name it by ear
• Build a streak and track your progress
• Choose exactly which chords you want to drill

BUILT-IN TUNER
• Tune by ear with clean reference tones, or
• Use the live microphone tuner to see if you're sharp or flat
• Works for guitar, piano, and more — with alternate tunings

WHY KORDAL
• Designed for both total beginners and serious players
• Clean, modern interface with multiple themes
• Everything is explained in a built-in Help & Tutorial section

Download Kordal and finally see — and hear — how music fits together.
```
