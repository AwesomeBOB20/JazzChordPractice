# Privacy Policy — Kord

**Effective date:** _[fill in before publishing, e.g. 2026-06-04]_

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Kord** mobile app ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

> **TL;DR:** The App works entirely on your device. We don't have accounts, we don't ask for your name or email, and we don't send your data to a server. The microphone is used only for the on-device guitar tuner, and audio is never recorded or transmitted.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.** Specifically:

### Microphone (tuner only)
On supported devices, the **Tuner → Listen** feature uses your microphone to detect the pitch of the note you play. This audio is analyzed **in real time on your device** to estimate the frequency. It is **never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere**, and the microphone is only active while you are actively using the Listen tuner.

### Data stored on your device
Your settings and progress are saved **locally on your device only** (using the operating system's standard app storage). This includes things like your theme, selected instrument, tempo, quiz progress/score, and any chord progressions you create. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the App.

### What we do **not** collect
- No name, email, phone number, or account information
- No location data
- No contacts, photos, or files
- No recordings of your microphone or audio

---

## 2. How We Use Information

Because all data stays on your device, it is used solely to make the App function — remembering your preferences, tracking your quiz progress, and powering the tuner. We do not profile you or use your data for any other purpose.

## 3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. _(See the Advertising section below if/when ads are enabled.)_

## 4. Children's Privacy

The App is a music-education tool suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, no special data is collected from children either.

## 5. Permissions

| Permission | Why | Required? |

| Microphone | Real-time pitch detection for the guitar tuner (Listen mode) | Optional — only the Listen tuner uses it |

You can deny or revoke microphone access in your device settings; the rest of the App (chords, scales, quiz, reference-tone tuner) will continue to work.
