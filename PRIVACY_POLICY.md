# Privacy Policy for Kordal

**Effective date:** June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kordal mobile application ("Kordal", "the app", "we", "us")
handles information when you use it. By using Kordal, you agree to the practices described here.

> **Contact:** kordalapp@gmail.com

---

## Summary (plain English)

- Kordal does **not** ask you to create an account and does **not** collect your name, email, or
  contacts.
- Your settings, saved songs, and quiz scores are stored **only on your device**.
- The **tuner microphone** is used **only** to detect pitch in real time, on your device. Audio is
  **never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.**
- The **free version shows ads** (Google AdMob) and offers a **one-time in-app purchase** to remove
  them (processed by the app store and RevenueCat). These services collect limited data as described
  below.

---

## 1. Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your device and is **not transmitted to us**:

- App settings and preferences (theme, instrument, tempo, labels, sound choices, etc.)
- Songs/progressions you create or save
- Quiz scores and progress
- Whether you have purchased the ad-free / Pro upgrade

You can erase all of this at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## 2. Microphone (Tuner)

The Tuner feature uses your device's microphone to measure the pitch of the sound it hears so it can
show you whether a note is in tune. This audio is processed **in real time on your device only**. It
is **not recorded, stored, or transmitted** to us or any third party. The microphone is active only
while you are using the Tuner's "Listen" mode.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Kordal displays ads through **Google AdMob**. To serve ads, Google may collect
and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device
information, and app-usage signals. This may be used for personalized or non-personalized advertising
depending on your consent and region.

- In the EU, EEA, UK, and other applicable regions, Kordal presents a **consent form** (via Google's
  User Messaging Platform) on first launch, and respects your choice.
- You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device's settings (e.g., "Ads" / "Reset
  advertising ID").

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Purchases (RevenueCat & the app store)

If you buy the ad-free / Pro upgrade, the transaction is handled by the **Google Play Store** (and,
where available, the Apple App Store). We use **RevenueCat** to verify and manage purchases.
RevenueCat receives a randomly generated, anonymous app-user identifier and purchase-related
information (such as the product purchased and purchase status) so the app can unlock your upgrade
and restore it on your devices. We do **not** receive your credit-card or payment details — those are
handled solely by the app store.

RevenueCat's Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
Google Play's terms apply to all purchases.

## 5. How information is used

Information processed by the third-party services above is used to:

- Display ads in the free version
- Provide and restore your in-app purchase
- Keep the app functioning and secure

We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. Data retention

We do not maintain our own servers or user database. Data held by Google AdMob and RevenueCat is
retained according to their respective policies. On-device data remains until you clear it or
uninstall the app.

## 7. Children's privacy

Kordal is intended for a general audience and is **not directed to children under 13** (or the
equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct,
delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because
Kordal does not maintain its own user database, most such requests are directed to the third-party
services above. For ad-related choices, use the in-app consent options (where shown) and your device
settings. For purchase records, contact the app store or RevenueCat. You may also contact us using
the email above and we will help where we can.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this page with an
updated "Effective date." Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
